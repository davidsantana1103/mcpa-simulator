# MCPA question-bank review

Read-only review of `../questions.js` against the official MuleSoft Platform Architect exam guide. `questions.js` is not modified.

- `REPORT_1_NON_ALIGNED_QUESTIONS.md` - questions that do not match the real exam (scope, format, facts), with reasons.
- `REPORT_2_TOPIC_DISTRIBUTION.md` - question count and % per exam topic and per exam objective.
- `REPORT_3_COVERAGE_VS_EXAM_WEIGHT.md` - bank share vs exam weights, gaps, and the questions needed to close them.

`judgments.py` holds the per-question review (objective + issue tags). Regenerate with `python3 reports/generate_reports.py`.
